AirpaneDesktop

Control your computer
with your hand.

Point at your webcam to move the cursor. Pinch to click and drag, make a V to scroll, swipe to change desktops. Everything else is yours to set: any gesture, any shortcut.

Install on a Mac: open Terminal, paste this, press Return
curl -fsSL https://airpane.anchit-tandon.com/desktop/install.sh | bash

Free. Takes a minute or two. Needs macOS with Python 3.9 to 3.12 (the installer tells you if it is missing). Video never leaves your Mac.

1

Install

Paste the command above in Terminal. After that, start Airpane from the Applications folder in your home folder.

2

Allow two things

Camera (macOS asks) and Accessibility for Terminal in System Settings > Privacy & Security, so your hand can move the mouse.

3

Point

Sit about an arm's length away, raise your index finger and move. The settings page shows what the camera sees.

16 gestures out of the box

Index finger up, moveMove the cursor
Thumb touches index, quicklyClick
Thumb touches index, moveDrag
Thumb touches middle fingerRight click
V sign, move up or downScroll
Open hand, swipe left or rightNext or previous desktop
Open hand, swipe up or downMission Control or show desktop
Three fingers, holdSwitch app
Thumbs up, holdVolume up
Thumbs down, holdVolume down
Index and pinky up, holdPlay or pause
Only the pinky up, holdMute
Four fingers, thumb folded inScreenshot
Fist, hold a secondPause or resume Airpane

Your rules

Every gesture above can be changed, and you can add your own on the settings page:

  • One gesture, one job: 12 hand poses and 4 swipes, each doing exactly one thing, so nothing fires by accident
  • Any action: clicks, scrolling, any keyboard shortcut, typing text, volume and music keys, Mission Control, desktops, screenshots, Spotlight
  • Timing: how long to hold, repeat while held, cooldown between uses
  • Feel: hand travel, steadiness, click speed, scroll speed and direction
  • Test mode: see what each gesture would do without touching your computer

Safe by design

  • Hold a fist or press Ctrl + Alt + P to pause at any moment
  • The mouse button is released if your hand leaves the camera mid-drag
  • Runs entirely on your Mac; the settings page is only reachable from it
  • Never runs commands, only clicks and keys you choose
