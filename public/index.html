Set up the glass

Lay the phone or iPad flat and lean a clear acrylic sheet over it at 45°, resting on the far edge and rising towards you.

2

Pair your laptop

On the laptop, open this page and press "Control it by hand from this laptop". Scan the QR code with the phone. Nothing to type.

3

Use it in the air

Move an open hand to move the cursor, make a fist to click, move your index finger up or down to scroll, flick it left or right to go back or forward.

Set up the floating page

  1. Cut 4 trapezoids from clear plastic: 6 cm wide at the bottom, 1 cm at the top, 3.5 cm tall. Printable template
  2. Tape the long edges together into a pyramid.
  3. Lay the phone flat, screen up, brightness high.
  4. Stand the pyramid upside down, small end on the + mark.
  5. Dim the room and look from the side, level with the phone. The page floats inside.

Tap the screen any time to bring back the controls. The floating page is for viewing; change the site from the address bar.
